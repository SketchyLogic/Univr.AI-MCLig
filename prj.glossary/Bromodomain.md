# Bromodomain

A protein domain (~110 amino acids) that recognises and binds acetylated lysine residues on histone proteins. Bromodomains are "readers" of the histone code — they act as molecular switches that control gene expression by recognising acetylation marks deposited by histone acetyltransferases.

**Pharmacological relevance:** BET bromodomain proteins (BRD2, BRD3, BRD4, BRDT) are involved in regulating oncogenes and inflammatory genes. BET inhibitors (e.g. JQ1) are a class of anti-cancer and anti-inflammatory drugs that compete with acetylated histones for bromodomain binding. The bromodomain was one of the first "epigenetic reader" domains to be successfully targeted by small molecules.
