# Boltz-2

An AI model (Passaro et al. 2025) that predicts the binding affinity of protein-ligand complexes, returning a predicted log(IC50) value. It is designed as a more accurate alternative to empirical scoring functions and MMGBSA for rapid affinity estimation.

**IC50** (half-maximal inhibitory concentration) measures how much of a compound is needed to inhibit a target by 50% — lower IC50 = higher potency. The model's log(IC50) predictions are considered comparable in accuracy to alchemical free energy calculations, which are the most rigorous computational affinity methods but extremely expensive.

**In AI-MCLig:** Boltz-2 serves as an independent validation of the compounds generated by the MC simulation. Because AI-MCLig uses Chai-1 confidence scores during design, using Boltz-2 (a different model with different assumptions) for post-hoc validation provides more confidence that the generated compounds are genuinely likely to be active.

**Availability:** Source code and usage at https://github.com/jwohlwend/boltz
